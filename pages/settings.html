Frequently Asked Questions

How do I discharge a patient and allocate their bed to another incoming emergency?

Navigate to the Bed Management tab, locate the assigned bed card, click "Change Status", select "Discharged / Cleaning Needed", and click Save. The bed will automatically refresh in the availability pool.

Where can I export HIPAA security audit logs for compliance review?

Go to Audit & Compliance, filter by date range or severity, and click the "Export CSV Log" button in the top right corner.
